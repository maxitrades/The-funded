FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the challenge fee refundable? ▾
Yes. Your challenge fee is fully refunded when you receive your first payout from the funded account. This means your entry cost is effectively zero once you prove your skills.
What instruments can I trade? ▾
You can trade all major and minor forex pairs, gold (XAUUSD), silver, major indices (NAS100, S&P500, DAX), cryptocurrencies (BTC, ETH), and commodities. All with leverage up to 1:100.
Is there a time limit to pass the challenge? ▾
No. There is no maximum time limit on any of our challenge programs. You only need to trade a minimum of 5 days. Take as long as you need to trade consistently and hit the target safely.
Can I use Expert Advisors (EAs)? ▾
Yes, EAs and automated trading bots are fully permitted on all programs. However, strategies that exploit platform latency, tick data, or arbitrage pricing are not allowed.
How does the scaling program work? ▾
After every 3 consecutive profitable months on your funded account, your account size is automatically increased. You can scale from your initial size all the way up to $200,000 in funded capital.
What are the payout methods? ▾
We support cryptocurrency withdrawals (USDT, Bitcoin, Ethereum) and international bank transfers. All payouts are processed within 48 hours of request. Minimum withdrawal amount is $50.
What happens if I breach a rule? ▾
If you breach the daily drawdown or maximum loss limit, your challenge or funded account is closed. You can purchase a new challenge at any time. Some programs offer a reset option at a discounted fee.
